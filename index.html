About me

Safe plants.
Solid websites.

I work shift operations at SPIE ETS, based at Hibiscus Petroleum, inside the onshore gas plant and out on the offshore wells. Amine treating, condensate stabilization, 3-phase separation, pigging and high-pressure equipment are my day-to-day.

Before the plant, I was a web developer and then Head of IT for a café group. Plant work taught me discipline: follow the procedure, watch the numbers, stay calm when things trip. I bring that to every project.
